# The Loli's Connection: A SIFE Operation
## Level 1 — Days 1-5: The Quad

### Setup
SIFE gets a $60 seed fund to run a Loli's taco table on the Quad.  
Rent of $180 is due to Mrs. West on Friday (Day 5).  
Tiny campus market: GOLD, TACO, NAPZ.  
University Star headlines drop daily clues.

---

## 1. Day-by-Day Story Beats

### Day 1
- Morning card: SIFE drops $60 seed money and one grill on the Quad. Matt duct-tapes the wobbly table before the first customer.
- End-of-day: Jared pitches gold before saying hello. Star asks if tacos are really back.

### Day 2
- Morning card: Ali starts the grill still muttering about the Mavericks. Heat already climbing.
- End-of-day: Grill slows while Ali rants. "They traded LUKA. On PURPOSE."

### Day 3
- Morning card: Move-in parents and RA Dana's freshman herd flood the Quad. Aaron gets hired mid-rush.
- End-of-day: Aaron hands Mrs. West a business card. She tucks it away "for kindling."

### Day 4
- Morning card: Ali switches targets to the Cowboys. Antonio quietly renames campus Wi-Fi LOLIS_FREE_TACOS.
- End-of-day: TACO looks ready to climb. Phillip tests the tortilla cannon on an empty bench.

### Day 5
- Morning card: Rent day. $180 due. Mrs. West arrives in person under the live oaks.
- End-of-day: "Lovely little patch, isn't it? Next one's pricier." Cash or short—her smile never changes.
